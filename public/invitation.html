Chapter one

Our story

Khuliso and Claire met in 2023 shortly after Khuliso had moved to Eugene, Oregon for school. Their shared devotion to God and serving His church allowed two unlikely paths to cross. Though they spent the first 20 years of their lives 10,000 miles apart, it soon became clear that the Lord had orchestrated their coming together. Khuliso and Claire started dating in August 2023, were engaged in South Africa in December 2024, and married in Eugene, Oregon on March 22, 2025. From the time they started dating, to the day they were married, and each day after, Jesus has been at the center of their relationship. Jesus has blessed their relationship in countless ways and they attribute all the glory of their relationship to Him. They couldn’t be more grateful for both sides of their family who supported them so fully. They look forward to celebrating their marriage with family and friends in South Africa this November.
